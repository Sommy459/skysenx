DATA ANALYSIS

Unlock the stories hidden in data! This course introduces participants to the basics of statistics and set theory, building a foundation for understanding data. They will learn how to use Microsoft Excel to organize, analyze, and visualize data through charts and formulas. Next, they will explore powerful tools like Tableau and Power BI to create interactive dashboards and reports that bring data to life and basics of MySQL to manipulate data. The course develops critical thinking and analytical skills essential for making informed decisions

WEB DEVELOPMENT

Turn ideas into real websites! In this course, participants will discover how the internet works and learn to create their own beautiful and interactive web pages from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. They will start by understanding the basics of web structure and style, then move on to making dynamic web pages with animations and interactive elements. Along the way, they’ll learn how to connect their sites to live data and explore how websites are hosted and accessed worldwide
